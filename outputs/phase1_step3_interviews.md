# Phase 1 / Step 3: Customer interviews (Mom Test)

**Rules:** Talk about their life, not your idea. Ask about specifics in the past, not opinions about the future. Talk less, listen more. Compliments and 'I would buy that' are not evidence; past behaviour and money/time already spent are.

StopGo does not contact anyone. The founder sends the outreach note personally and records results.

## Questions
1. Tell me about the last time you ran into this problem. What happened?
2. How did you deal with it? What did you try?
3. How much time did that take you? How often does it happen?
4. Have you looked for or paid for anything to fix it? Why / why not?
5. What is the hardest part about it?
6. Who else on your team deals with this?
7. Is there anyone else you think I should talk to?
